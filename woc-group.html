WOC-Foundation

WOC-Group

The internal personnel structure of the Foundation — from support staff to the Directorate. Each rank carries a designated insignia and defines access level, responsibility, and operational authority.

TE-P

Test Exposure Personnel

Personnel assigned to controlled tests, contact attempts, and monitored exposures. Operates under strict protocol with full medical oversight.

SRU

Sanitation & Recovery Unit

Cleaners

Cleaning, trace removal, decontamination, and the disposal of occult residues. Ensures sites remain sterile and free of anomalous contamination after operations.

C-1P

Class-1 Personnel

General staff — IT, kitchen, craftsmen, and administrative support. No direct access to anomalies or restricted containment areas.

C-2P

Class-2 Personnel

Lab personnel, researchers, analysts, and archivists with limited access to contained subjects under supervised conditions.

SP

Security Personnel

Security staff responsible for protecting WOC sites, enforcing access control, and maintaining perimeter integrity at all Foundation facilities.

OPF

Occult Protection Force

Special units and tactical intervention forces — highly trained military personnel deployed for active containment breaches and field operations.

C-3P

Class-3 Personnel

Senior researchers, professors, department heads, and project leads. Holds authority over research divisions and containment evaluation protocols.

C-4P

Class-4 Personnel

Administrative senior leadership — site directors, regional command, and operational oversight of entire Foundation facilities and their personnel.

C-5P

Class-5 Personnel

Special authorised personnel — holders of exceptional clearance with access to all inquiries, records, and restricted Foundation operations across every division.

WOC-DIR

WOC Directorate

The international leadership of the World Ocultism Containment Foundation. Highest authority — directs global operations, policy, and strategic containment decisions.
