Personnel Overview

The Dimensional Research Corporation brings science, field operations, security, and archival intelligence into one controlled system. Every role supports a single objective: understand what lies beyond our reality without allowing it to threaten the world we know.

DIVISION 01 · FIELD EXPLORATION

The teams that cross the threshold.

Our expedition personnel enter newly discovered dimensions before permanent infrastructure exists. They establish safe approach routes, document environmental conditions, recover verified samples, and maintain continuous contact with Central Command. Every deployment combines scientific observation with disciplined field procedure, because a world that appears stable can change without warning.

Primary MissionSurvey, mapping and first-contact assessment
Operational StandardThree-person units with independent extraction capability
“We do not enter to conquer. We enter to understand — and to return.”
DIVISION 02 · DIMENSIONAL SCIENCES

Research that makes contact survivable.

Recovered organisms, minerals, atmospheric particles, and microbial cultures are examined inside controlled DRC laboratories. Researchers identify biological hazards, unusual physical properties, and interactions that do not follow terrestrial models. Their findings determine whether an environment can be revisited, what protection future teams require, and which discoveries must remain permanently isolated.

Research ScopeMicrobiology, xenochemistry and material analysis
Safety ProtocolSealed sample chains with continuous verification
“Every sample is evidence from a reality governed by different rules.”
DIVISION 03 · SECURITY & CONTAINMENT

Protection without hesitation.

DRC security officers protect research sites, dimensional gates, personnel, and classified materials. They are trained for facility defense, controlled recovery operations, and encounters with entities whose behavior cannot be predicted by ordinary threat models. Their presence is deliberate and measured: force is a final safeguard, not a substitute for procedure.

DeploymentGate security, escort duty and containment response
EquipmentMission-specific protection and monitored field systems
“Containment begins long before an alarm is triggered.”
DIVISION 04 · CENTRAL ARCHIVES

Every world leaves a record.

The Central Archives preserve the complete operational history of the Corporation: expedition logs, cartographic revisions, biological reports, entity profiles, recovered communications, and incident reviews. Each file is cross-referenced by dimension, risk level, and chain of custody, ensuring that no team enters an environment without access to everything previously learned there.

Archive ClassesDimensions, entities, artifacts and incidents
Access ControlRole-based clearance with immutable audit records
“Memory is our first line of defense against repetition.”

The Dimensional Research Corporation is an independent scientific and operational institution dedicated to the controlled discovery, study, and containment of realities beyond our own. We unite researchers, field teams, security personnel, and archivists under one command structure so that every crossing is documented, every risk is evaluated, and every return is accounted for.

01

Who we are

DRC personnel work across science, exploration, logistics, intelligence, medical support, and containment. Each division operates independently in the field but contributes to one verified archive and one shared operational picture.

02

Our objective

Our primary objective is to identify stable dimensional access points and build a reliable understanding of the worlds behind them. Discovery alone is never considered success; knowledge must be repeatable, traceable, and safe to use.

03

Our duty

Every object, organism, signal, and returning team is subject to controlled review. When a dimension presents a danger to personnel or our world, the DRC restricts access, isolates the hazard, and maintains containment for as long as required.

Discover. Research. Contain.

These principles define the order of every DRC operation. We discover without assuming safety. We research without compromising evidence. We contain without hesitation when the integrity of our world is at risk. No division operates outside this mandate, and no dimensional record is considered complete until its consequences are understood.
